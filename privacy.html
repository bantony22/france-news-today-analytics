Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. About the Service

France News Today Analytics is a private analytics tool designed to retrieve and analyze data associated with authorized TikTok accounts for content performance analysis and management.

2. Information We Access

With the user's authorization, the service may access information made available through TikTok's official APIs, including basic profile information, account statistics, and information about public videos and their performance.

3. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through TikTok is used solely for analytics, content performance monitoring, reporting, and optimization of the authorized account's content strategy.

4. TikTok Authorization

TikTok data is accessed only after the account holder explicitly authorizes the application through TikTok's official authentication system. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the user.

5. Data Sharing

Data obtained through TikTok is not sold or provided to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and data accessed through the service.

7. Revoking Access

Users may revoke the application's access to their TikTok account through TikTok's account settings. After access is revoked, the service will no longer be able to retrieve new data from the account.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service or its use of TikTok APIs changes.

9. Contact

Questions concerning this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the official France News Today account.
